## Notes

- Ubigi has no 1 GB tier for New Zealand — smallest single-country plan is 3 GB/30 d.
- Jetpac and GigSky country pages returned HTTP 403/404 during this research pass — omitted rather than guessed. Worth re-checking later.
- Maya Mobile sells only a global flat-rate unlimited tier (identical price in every country, see [providers.md](../providers.md)).
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled and **hotspot is frequently blocked** — confirm before buying. (Its NZ page showed the same USD price ladder as its Australia page.)
