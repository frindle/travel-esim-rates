## Coverage notes

- **Airalo "Oceania"** — the product page states "8 Countries and Networks" but does not list them
  individually on the fetched page or the general coverage page (which 404'd on re-check). Not
  independently enumerated here — treat the country list as unconfirmed pending a direct spot-check.
- **Holafly "Oceania"** — 11 countries confirmed on-page: **Australia, Fiji, Indonesia, Malaysia,
  New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, Singapore, Thailand, Tonga, Vanuatu, Vietnam**. Note this bundle is
  not pure Oceania — it blends in several Southeast Asian countries (Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore,
  Thailand, Vietnam), so it's really an "Oceania + SEA" bundle sold under the Oceania name. Plan
  includes an "Always On" 1 GB/month backup data benefit valid across 150+ countries (separate from
  the regional allowance).
- **aloSIM** — confirmed to sell **9 individual Oceania destinations** (Australia, New Zealand, Fiji,
  French Polynesia, Guam, Papua New Guinea, Kiribati, Tonga, Nauru) but its Oceania page describes
  per-country plans only ("Plans start from just $4.00") — **no unified multi-country regional
  bundle found**. Not included as a regional row.
- **Saily** — no Oceania-specific regional plan page found (`saily.com/esim-plans/oceania/` and
  `saily.com/esim-oceania/` both 404). Not included; may exist under a different URL not found this
  pass.
- **Maya Mobile, Nomad, Ubigi, Jetpac, GigSky** — no dedicated Oceania/South Pacific regional
  bundle page could be located/fetched this pass (404s or no plan content returned). Maya Mobile's
  general **Global unlimited** tiers (see [Global/README.md](../Global/README.md)) would cover
  Oceania as part of their worldwide plan, so they are not omitted entirely — just not a
  region-specific SKU.
