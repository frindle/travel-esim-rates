## Carrier resolution

## Notes

- Fiji runs noticeably more expensive per GB than Australia/NZ across nearly every major provider (island/Pacific surcharge) — worth flagging to travelers.
- Saily and Ubigi have no Fiji-specific 5 GB/30 d single-country plan; closest single-country tiers and one clearly-marked multi-country fallback are shown instead.
- Jetpac's Fiji page returned HTTP 403 during this research pass — omitted rather than guessed (Jetpac's Australia rates are in [Australia.md](Australia.md) for reference).
- Maya Mobile sells only a global flat-rate unlimited tier (identical price in every country).
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled and **hotspot is frequently blocked** — confirm before buying.
