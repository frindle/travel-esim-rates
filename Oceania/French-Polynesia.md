## Notes

- **This is the most expensive Oceania destination in this dataset by a wide margin** — even a bare 1 GB/7 d plan runs $8.50–$16 across every major provider, vs. $4–8 in Australia/NZ/Fiji. Remote-island surcharge; confirm current pricing before booking.
- Nomad, Maya Mobile, GigSky's own site, and Jetpac's per-provider eSIMDB pages returned **HTTP 403** during this research pass — omitted rather than guessed. Re-check later; a WebSearch snippet (not independently fetched) suggested Ubigi may also sell a 25 GB/30 d plan around $79, but it's **not included here** since no page could be directly verified for it.
- Holafly's French Polynesia page priced two capped plans (1 GB/5 d, 2 GB/7 d) in EUR despite branding itself "unlimited data" elsewhere — likely a mismatched/legacy listing. USD figures above are an approximate FX conversion (⚠️), not a price Holafly itself quoted in USD.
