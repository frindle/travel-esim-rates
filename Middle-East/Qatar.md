## Notes

- Maya Mobile is a flat-rate global unlimited product (same $9.99/$19.99/$27.99/$49.99 tiers in every one of its 165+ countries) — the price is identical to what's listed for Turkey and Jordan in this repo.
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled and hotspot/tethering is frequently blocked on its plans generally (per [providers.md](../providers.md)) — confirm hotspot support before buying if you need it.
- No Qatar-specific eSIM/VoIP regulatory restrictions were surfaced in this research pass (unlike some neighboring Gulf states with VoIP-app blocking rules) — not independently verified against Qatari telecom regulation, so treat as absence of evidence rather than confirmation.
