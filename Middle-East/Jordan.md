## Carrier resolution

- **Airalo** → Zain JO — stated directly on Airalo's Jordan eSIM page
- **Nomad** → Zain JO — stated directly on Nomad's Jordan eSIM page ("all plans connect to the zain JO network")
- **Holafly** → Zain Jordan — stated directly on Holafly's Jordan eSIM page ("4G/5G connectivity through Zain Jordan network")

## Notes

- Nomad's Jordan eSIM store page (edelweiss.nomadesim.com) only displayed prices in CHF at fetch time — the ~$5 figure above is the site's own USD headline ("from US$ 5"), not a computed conversion, so it's flagged ⚠️ approx/"from" rather than an exact per-GB plan price.
- No sourced Airalo unlimited or Nomad 5 GB rate could be confirmed for Jordan within this research pass's budget (Airalo Jordan's catalog is capped-GB only, up to 20 GB/30 d — no unlimited tier is offered, unlike its Qatar/Turkey catalogs); aloSIM likewise lists no priced unlimited tier for Jordan ("Unlimited — price not specified" on its own page).
- Maya Mobile is a flat-rate global unlimited product (same $9.99/$19.99/$27.99/$49.99 tiers everywhere) — identical pricing to what's listed for Qatar and Turkey in this repo.
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled and hotspot/tethering is frequently blocked on its plans generally (per [providers.md](../providers.md)) — confirm hotspot support before buying if you need it.
- No Jordan-specific eSIM/VoIP regulatory restrictions were surfaced in this research pass.
