## Coverage notes

- **Airalo "Middle East and North Africa"** — the fetched product page states the bundle spans
  **"13 Countries and Networks"** but does not enumerate them on the page itself; the individual
  country list would need a follow-up fetch of Airalo's coverage/device-compatibility sub-page.
  Both "Middle East" and "North Africa" are in the bundle name, so it likely includes some African
  countries (e.g. Egypt, Morocco) alongside Gulf/Levant states — treat as broader than a pure
  "Middle East" bundle.
- **Holafly "Middle East"** — 11 named countries confirmed on-page: **Armenia, Azerbaijan, Cyprus,
  Egypt, Israel, Jordan, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, United Arab Emirates**. Notably this
  omits Bahrain and Kuwait (both have single-country pages in this repo) and includes several
  countries not usually classed as "Middle East" in a strict sense (Armenia, Azerbaijan, Cyprus).
- No sourceable regional bundle was found for Saily, Nomad, Ubigi, aloSIM, Maya Mobile, Jetpac, or
  GigSky for the Middle East — their regional/Middle-East URLs either 404'd or the fetched page had
  no plan data (Jetpac's Middle East product page loads JS-rendered pricing not present in a plain
  fetch). Omitted rather than guessed, per repo rule.
- Hotspot: Holafly is widely reported to block/limit hotspot on many unlimited plans (see
  [providers.md](../providers.md)) — marked ⚠️ here since it wasn't independently reconfirmed for
  this specific bundle, only carried over from the provider-level trait.
