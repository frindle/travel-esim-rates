## Notes

- Jetpac and GigSky were checked but no sourced Oman pricing was found on their public pages during this research pass — omitted rather than guessed.
- Airalo's Oman page badge ("from $4.00 USD") implies a fixed-GB/capped plan tier exists, but that tier is loaded client-side by JavaScript and wasn't retrievable via fetch — only the Unlimited-tier prices above are sourced for Airalo here.
- **VoIP note (sourced):** per [Wikipedia — Voice over IP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_over_IP), Oman has historically treated unauthorized/unlicensed VoIP services as illegal, with unlicensed VoIP-provider websites blocked and penalties reported up to 50,000 OMR and/or two years' imprisonment (citing a 2009 enforcement sweep). This evidence is dated, not a live 2026 confirmation — verify current WhatsApp/Skype voice-call status with Oman's Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (TRA) before relying on VoIP apps for calls.
- Ubigi is data-only (no voice/SMS) — see [providers.md](../providers.md).
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled and hotspot is frequently blocked on its plans generally — confirm before buying (see [providers.md](../providers.md)).
