## Notes

- **Regulatory quirk:** WhatsApp and Skype **voice/video calls are blocked by UAE telecom regulation on all networks**, including on eSIM data — this applies regardless of provider. Data-based messaging (WhatsApp text, iMessage) still works. (Source: [eSIMRated — Best eSIM UAE](https://esimrated.com/en/best-esim-for-uae).)
- Unlike the general Holafly hotspot-blocking caveat in [providers.md](../providers.md), the UAE Holafly plan explicitly advertises a 1 GB/day hotspot-sharing allowance — confirm at purchase time since Holafly policies vary by destination.
- The only two national mobile networks in the UAE are **Etisalat (e&)** and **du**; virtually all eSIM providers resolve to one of these two.
- GigSky and Jetpac exact UAE plan pages returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch (esimdb.com); only promo/rating data could be sourced — revisit with a browser-based fetch if precise figures are needed.
