## Carrier resolution

- **Airalo** → stc Bahrain (primary) + 1 other network option
- **Holafly** → stc Bahrain
- **Nomad** → stc network
- **Saily**, **Ubigi**, **aloSIM**, **Maya Mobile** → local carrier not disclosed on the fetched provider page; check at checkout.

## Notes

- Jetpac and GigSky were checked but no sourced Bahrain pricing was found on their public pages during this research pass — omitted rather than guessed.
- Airalo's Bahrain page only exposed Unlimited-tier pricing via fetch (the fixed-GB "Data" tab is JavaScript-rendered and wasn't retrievable) — only Unlimited plans are listed for Airalo here.
- No sourced information was found during this pass on VoIP/OTT call restrictions (WhatsApp/Skype) specific to Bahrain — unlike Oman and the UAE, no fetched source confirmed a current or historical VoIP-blocking regime here. Do not assume Oman/UAE-style restrictions apply without checking Bahrain's Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (TRA) guidance directly.
- Ubigi is data-only (no voice/SMS) — see [providers.md](../providers.md).
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled and hotspot is frequently blocked on its plans generally — confirm before buying (see [providers.md](../providers.md)).
