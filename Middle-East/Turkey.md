## Carrier resolution

- **Airalo** → Türk Telekom (Avea) — stated directly on Airalo's Turkey eSIM page
- **Nomad** → Avea — stated directly on Nomad's Turkey eSIM page ("service provider is Avea")
- **Türk Telekom (local)** row above is a direct Türk Telekom-branded eSIM (via eSIMRated), not a reseller
- **Saily / Ubigi / Holafly / aloSIM / Maya Mobile** → not confirmed on the pages fetched; Turkey's major MNOs are Turkcell, Vodafone Turkey, and Türk Telekom, so most reseller eSIMs likely resolve to one of these three

## Notes

- Ubigi's Turkey plan page and the eSIMRated Türk Telekom page both note **IMEI registration in Turkey applies only to devices used in-country for more than 120 consecutive days** — short-stay tourist eSIM travel is unaffected by Turkey's device-registration rules. No mention of a broader eSIM-provider ban was found in the sources checked, despite that claim surfacing in some secondary/aggregated search summaries — treat any "Turkey has banned provider X" claim as unverified until seen on a primary source.
- Maya Mobile is a flat-rate global unlimited product (same $9.99/$19.99/$27.99/$49.99 tiers everywhere) — identical pricing to what's listed for Qatar and Jordan in this repo.
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled and hotspot/tethering is frequently blocked on its plans generally (per [providers.md](../providers.md)) — confirm hotspot support before buying if you need it.
