## Notes

- Israel's eSIM market is unusually **GB-plan-only for the value providers** (Saily, Nomad, aloSIM all list capped GB tiers), while **Airalo, Holafly, and Maya only offer unlimited day-passes** for Israel specifically — a different mix than typical Airalo catalogs elsewhere.
- aloSIM includes a free Hushed US/local phone number with Israel plans (useful for hotel check-in texts / WhatsApp/app sign-up verification).
- No confirmed Ubigi Israel plan page was found — Ubigi's general destination list includes Israel, but per-plan pricing could not be located/fetched at research time.
- No unusual eSIM/VoIP regulatory restrictions (of the UAE/Oman/Kuwait type) were found for Israel in this research pass.
