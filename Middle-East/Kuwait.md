## Notes

- Jetpac and GigSky were checked but no sourced Kuwait pricing was found on their public pages during this research pass — omitted rather than guessed.
- Airalo's Kuwait page only exposed Unlimited-tier pricing via fetch (the fixed-GB "Data" tab is JavaScript-rendered and wasn't retrievable) — only Unlimited plans are listed for Airalo here.
- Holafly's Kuwait page did not list a 30-day option (longest found was 15 d / $58.90) — may be a gap in what the fetch captured, not necessarily unavailable from Holafly.
- No sourced current (2026) information was found on VoIP/OTT call restrictions (WhatsApp/Skype) specific to Kuwait during this pass. Gulf states have had past reporting of restrictions on VoIP calling apps (see Oman's note in [Middle-East/Oman.md](Oman.md)), but no page fetched here confirmed Kuwait's current status — do not assert a restriction without checking Kuwait's Communication and Information Technology Regulatory Authority (CITRA) guidance directly.
- Ubigi is data-only (no voice/SMS) — see [providers.md](../providers.md).
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled and hotspot is frequently blocked on its plans generally — confirm before buying (see [providers.md](../providers.md)).
