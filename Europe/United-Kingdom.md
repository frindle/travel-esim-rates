## Notes

- GigSky's UK plan page is JS-rendered and returned no plan data via fetch — omitted rather than guessed.
- Airalo and Holafly UK pages show only unlimited day-pass tiers on their own sites (no small fixed-GB plans surfaced); Airalo's own capped "EUconnect/Uki Mobile" sub-plans (1 GB from $4.00, 5 GB from $12.00) were seen via a third-party aggregator (esimdb.com) but that source returned inconsistent access (403) on re-check, so only the directly-fetched Airalo unlimited rate is kept here.
- Maya Mobile is unlimited-tier-only in the UK (no metered/GB plans found).
- Europe-wide regional plans (see [Europe index](README.md)) may beat single-country UK pricing for multi-country trips, though the UK is now outside the EU roaming zone so some "EU+UK" bundles apply.
