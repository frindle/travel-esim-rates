## Notes

- Ubigi, Jetpac, and GigSky: no sourced Ireland-specific rate found. Ubigi's per-country plan URLs (which worked for Portugal) 404'd for Ireland; Jetpac's domain did not resolve; GigSky's site is JS-rendered and did not expose plan data. Omitted rather than guessed.
- Airalo's capped (1 GB/5 GB) pricing came from esimdb.com's Airalo catalog listing for Ireland, since airalo.com/ireland-esim's fetched view only surfaced its unlimited tier.
- aloSIM's page also listed a 7-day "Unlimited" plan at $5.00, well below its own 10-day ($11.50) and every other country's 7-day unlimited price (~$11-19) — this looked like a data-extraction anomaly rather than a real price, so it was excluded; the 10-day figure was used instead.
- Saily's "Unlimited" plans are 5 GB/day high-speed then throttled to ~1 Mbps, not a hard cap but not truly unlimited.
- A Europe-wide regional plan (Airalo Eurolink, Holafly Europe, etc.) may beat single-country Ireland pricing for multi-country trips — see the [Europe index](README.md).
