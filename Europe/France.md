## Notes

- Ubigi's row is carried over from the Japan-seed dataset (same $/GB tier) and is NOT confirmed France-specific — treat as ⚠️ until a France Ubigi page is fetched directly.
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled and hotspot is frequently blocked/limited — confirm before buying.
- Airalo's cheapest France plan (Élan/EUconnect 1 GB/3 d) is $4.00; at the 5 GB tier, Nomad ($9.00/30 d) and aloSIM ($9.00/30 d) beat Airalo ($10.00/7 d) on $/GB.
- Europe-wide regional plans (see [Europe index](README.md)) are likely competitive here for multi-country trips.
