## Notes

- Ubigi, Jetpac, and GigSky: no sourced Austria-specific rate found. Ubigi's per-country plan URLs (which worked for Portugal) 404'd for Austria; Jetpac's domain did not resolve; GigSky's site is JS-rendered and did not expose plan data. Omitted rather than guessed.
- Unlike some other countries, aloSIM's Austria page did not show a priced "unlimited" plan (mentioned as "available with separate terms" but no figure given) — omitted for that reason.
- Saily's "Unlimited" plans are 5 GB/day high-speed then throttled to ~1 Mbps, not a hard cap but not truly unlimited.
- A Europe-wide regional plan (Airalo Eurolink, Holafly Europe, etc.) may beat single-country Austria pricing for multi-country trips — see the [Europe index](README.md).
