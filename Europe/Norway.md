## Notes

- Airalo currently sells only unlimited-data plans for Norway — no fixed 1GB/5GB tiers were found on its site despite the page offering a "fixed data" filter option; hotspot/tethering support for these plans is not stated on the page, hence marked ⚠️.
- Ubigi's Norway price was found via the eSIMdb aggregator only ("10GB • 7 Days" listed as its #1 popular plan there); Ubigi's own site (ubigi.com / cellulardata.ubigi.com) is JS-rendered and did not expose plan details to a direct fetch, so this figure is marked ⚠️ pending confirmation from Ubigi's own page.
- aloSIM's "Unlimited" tiers are sold as a per-day rate (e.g. $3.50/day when buying a 30-day trip) rather than one flat price — shown here as the per-day rate rather than a single total.
- No sourced rates were found for Nomad, Holafly, Jetpac, or GigSky in Norway despite fetching their own sites and third-party aggregators (esimdb.com, esimtips.com, esimrated.com). GigSky's only Norway listing found on eSIMdb was a 100MB/7-day $0.00 promotional-size entry, too small and anomalous to treat as a comparable rate, so it was omitted rather than guessed at.
- No standalone national-carrier (Telenor/Telia) tourist eSIM data plan was found with sourced pricing; only the Airalo↔Telia network partnership above was confirmed.
- Europe-wide regional eSIM plans (see the Europe index) may beat single-country Norway pricing for short multi-country trips.
