## Notes

- Ubigi's Netherlands plan page did not expose plan pricing via fetch (the eSIM store loads dynamically and the direct plan-URL pattern that worked for UK/Germany 404'd for Netherlands) — omitted rather than guessed. Ubigi does list Netherlands among its 200+ covered destinations.
- GigSky's Netherlands plan page is JS-rendered and returned no plan data via fetch — omitted rather than guessed.
- Airalo and Holafly show only unlimited day-pass tiers on their own Netherlands pages; no small fixed-GB Airalo plan was independently confirmed.
- Maya Mobile is unlimited-tier-only in the Netherlands (no metered/GB plans found).
- Europe-wide regional plans (see [Europe index](README.md)) are likely competitive here for short trips.
