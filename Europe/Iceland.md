## Notes

- Airalo and Holafly for Iceland are unlimited-day-pass only (no metered GB tiers as of this research), so $/GB is not meaningfully computable.
- Could not confirm sourced pricing for Saily, Maya Mobile, Jetpac, or GigSky for Iceland after a real search effort (site searches 404'd or returned no Iceland-specific plan page); omitted rather than guessed.
- Hotspot support was not explicitly confirmed on any fetched Iceland plan page — marked ⚠️ pending verification. Per [providers.md](../providers.md), Holafly is known to block hotspot on many plans (especially in Europe), so treat its Iceland plan as ⚠️ until confirmed otherwise.
- Europe-wide regional plans (see the [Europe index](README.md)) may beat single-country pricing for short trips, especially for multi-country Nordic itineraries.
