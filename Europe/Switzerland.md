## Notes

- Switzerland (non-EU) is consistently priced higher than the EU countries in this repo across Saily, Nomad, and aloSIM's capped plans — confirms it doesn't inherit EU roaming-zone pricing.
- Jetpac's Switzerland catalog only listed 20/30/40 GB and unlimited 30-day tiers (no 5 GB or smaller option found), unlike its UK/Germany/Netherlands pages which had a 5 GB tier — used the smallest available (20 GB) instead.
- GigSky's Switzerland plan page is JS-rendered and returned no plan data via fetch — omitted rather than guessed.
- Airalo and Holafly show only unlimited day-pass tiers on their own Switzerland pages; no small fixed-GB Airalo plan was independently confirmed.
- Maya Mobile is unlimited-tier-only in Switzerland (no metered/GB plans found).
- Europe-wide regional plans (see [Europe index](README.md)) may not include Switzerland at EU-zone pricing since it's outside the EU — check the regional plan's country list before assuming it beats these single-country rates.
