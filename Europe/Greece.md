## Notes

- Ubigi, Jetpac, and GigSky: no sourced Greece-specific rate found. Ubigi's per-country plan URLs (which worked for Portugal) 404'd for Greece; Jetpac's domain did not resolve; GigSky's site is JS-rendered and did not expose plan data. Omitted rather than guessed.
- Airalo's capped (1 GB/5 GB) pricing came from esimdb.com's Airalo catalog listing for Greece, since airalo.com/greece-esim's fetched view only surfaced its unlimited tier.
- Saily's "Unlimited" plans are 5 GB/day high-speed then throttled to ~1 Mbps, not a hard cap but not truly unlimited.
- A Europe-wide regional plan (Airalo Eurolink, Holafly Europe, etc.) may beat single-country Greece pricing for multi-country trips — see the [Europe index](README.md).
