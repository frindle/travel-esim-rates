## Notes

- Jetpac and GigSky: no fetchable Portugal-specific pricing found (Jetpac's domain did not resolve during research; GigSky's site is JS-rendered and did not expose plan data to a page fetch). Omitted rather than guessed.
- Airalo's country landing page defaults to showing "unlimited" plans; capped 1 GB/5 GB pricing was sourced from esimdb.com's Airalo catalog listing for Portugal instead of airalo.com directly.
- Saily's "Unlimited" plans are actually 5 GB/day high-speed then throttled to ~1 Mbps — not a hard cap, but not truly unlimited either.
- A Europe-wide regional plan (Airalo Eurolink, Holafly Europe, etc.) may beat single-country Portugal pricing for multi-country trips — see the [Europe index](README.md).
