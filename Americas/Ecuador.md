## Carrier resolution

- **Nomad** → Movistar
- **Holafly** → Movistar Ecuador
- **Airalo** ("Bacanet" branded unlimited line) → Otecel (the registered network-operator name behind Movistar Ecuador)
- Saily, Maya Mobile, aloSIM, and Jetpac do not publish which local carrier (Claro/Movistar) their Ecuador eSIMs resolve to on the pages checked.

## Notes

- Airalo's Ecuador catalog is branded "Bacanet" (local); it also sells "Latamlink" (17-country regional) and "Discover"/"Discover+" (global) plans that include Ecuador — those regional/global variants are cheaper per-GB at some tiers but are not Ecuador-exclusive, so only the local Bacanet line is tabled above.
- Holafly and Maya Mobile are unlimited-only in Ecuador — no capped GB tiers found. Holafly's hotspot sharing is capped at 1 GB/day even though the base plan is unshared unlimited.
- Jetpac's Ecuador "Unlimited" 30-day pack ($270) is far more expensive than any other provider's unlimited offering; its throttling note states "first 3 GB every 24 hours at standard network speeds" before reduced speeds kick in.
- No sourced Saily 5 GB/30-day or unlimited plan was found specifically for Ecuador (only a 1 GB/7-day local plan and an unrelated global 20 GB/365-day plan were listed) — omitted rather than guessed.
- Ubigi: researched but its Ecuador plan page returned a 404 as of 2026-09-17; no confirmed pricing located. GigSky: researched but no confirmed Ecuador-specific pricing located.
- No Galápagos-specific coverage caveats were found in the sources checked (Holafly's Ecuador page made no mention of Galápagos); assume mainland-network coverage claims do not necessarily extend to the islands until confirmed.
