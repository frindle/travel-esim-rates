- **Nomad** → Movistar
- **aloSIM** → Telefónica Móviles Chile (i.e. the Movistar network)

## Notes

- Airalo and Jetpac "Unlimited"/high-tier plans are FUP-throttled (Airalo: 3 GB/day full speed then 1 Mbps; Jetpac: 3 GB/24h then ~1 Mbps) — not true unlimited.
- Holafly's hotspot is limited to a separate "Always On" 1 GB/day data-sharing feature, not full tethering like Saily/Nomad/Ubigi.
- Chile has four carriers — Movistar, Claro, Entel, WOM — with Movistar generally having the widest coverage and Entel the highest data speeds; most eSIM providers surveyed here resolve to Movistar/Telefónica. Coverage in remote regions (Atacama, Patagonia) can vary by carrier, so verify before relying on it off the beaten path.
- GigSky and Maya Mobile figures above come from aggregator/search summaries rather than a directly fetched pricing table for the exact plan tier requested — treat as approximate and re-verify before purchase.
