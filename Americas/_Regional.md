# Americas — Regional (Multi-Country) Plans

Multi-country eSIM plans that cover a **defined Americas sub-region** — Caribbean, Latin America
(a Central+South America blend as sold by providers), Central America, or North America — on a
single eSIM. These are **not** single-country plans (see `<Country>.md` in this folder) and **not**
worldwide/global plans (see [Global/README.md](../Global/README.md), e.g. Maya Mobile's global
unlimited tiers or Saily/Airalo/Nomad "from" catalog floors — those are excluded here).

Coverage is expressed per row via the leading **Coverage** column: sub-region label + the country
count as stated on the provider's own regional plan page. Providers scope "Latin America" differently
(see Coverage notes below) — counts are NOT interchangeable between providers even under the same
label. Rating = provider Trustpilot (see [providers.md](../providers.md)).

## Coverage notes

- **Caribbean**: Airalo's 26-country footprint and aloSIM's 20-country footprint were not itemized
  on-page beyond the count. Holafly's 18-country Caribbean list (confirmed on-page): Antigua and
  Barbuda plus others "ranging from Antigua and Barbuda to Turks and Caicos" — the full 18-name list
  was not returned verbatim by the fetch, only the count and endpoints; aloSIM's 20-country list
  *was* itemized: Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda, Aruba, Bahamas, Barbados, British Virgin Islands,
  Cayman Islands, Curaçao, Dominica, Dominican Republic, Grenada, Jamaica, Montserrat, Netherlands
  Antilles, Puerto Rico, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Turks
  and Caicos, U.S. Virgin Islands. Jetpac's page did not disclose a country count at all (an
  expandable "view all covered countries" section wasn't captured by the fetch).
- **Latin America — provider scoping differs, do not treat counts as comparable**:
  - **Holafly (18 countries, itemized)**: Argentina, Belize, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Costa
    Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua, Panama, Paraguay, Peru,
    Puerto Rico, Uruguay — i.e. Holafly's "Latin America" spans both Central America *and* South
    America (overlaps with its separate Central America and Caribbean products for Puerto Rico).
  - **Saily (12 countries, itemized)**: Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica,
    Ecuador, El Salvador, Mexico, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay — a narrower blend, missing several Central
    American and Caribbean-adjacent countries Holafly includes (Belize, Guatemala, Honduras,
    Nicaragua, Panama, Puerto Rico).
  - **Airalo (17 countries)**: count only — the fetch did not return an itemized list.
  - Net: "Latin America" is a marketing label, not a fixed set — a build.py consumer must key
    coverage per (provider, plan) pair, not assume all "Latin America" rows cover the same countries.
- **Central America (Holafly, 6 countries, itemized)**: Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras,
  Nicaragua, Panama.
- **North America (3 countries, both providers)**: United States, Canada, Mexico (Airalo's page
  confirmed the count only as "3 Countries and Networks" without naming them; Holafly's page named
  Canada, Mexico, USA explicitly).

## Notes

- Only plans the provider itself scopes to a sub-region (not global/worldwide) are listed here.
  Global-scope entries for these same providers (e.g. Airalo/Saily/Nomad "from" floor prices, Maya
  Mobile global unlimited) live in [Global/README.md](../Global/README.md) and are not duplicated.
  StaffTraveler is likewise excluded — per [Global/README.md](../Global/README.md) its 5 tiers are a
  single flat global SKU, not a distinct regional product.
  StaffTraveler's flat tiers happen to cover many Americas countries within its 168+ country footprint,
  but that is Global's remit, not this page's.
- Ubigi, Nomad, Maya Mobile, and GigSky did not return a fetchable Americas-region (Caribbean /
  Latin America / Central America / North America) plan page during this pass (404s or JS-rendered
  pages with no plan data on plain fetch) — omitted rather than guessed. Nomad in particular is known
  (see country pages) to sell only per-country plans in the Americas; no evidence of a Nomad regional
  bundle was found.
- Jetpac's Caribbean 20 GB tier ($100, pricier than its own 30 GB tier at $69.99) is very likely a
  stale/misconfigured price on Jetpac's site — flagged ⚠️ above, consistent with the existing Jetpac
  pricing-anomaly caveat in [providers.md](../providers.md).
- All prices fetched 2026-09-17 directly from each provider's own regional plan page (see Source
  column); ratings are the existing Trustpilot figures already recorded in
  [providers.md](../providers.md), not independently re-verified this pass.
