## Notes

- Airalo's Colombia catalog is branded "Chimba" (local) / "Chimba+" (local, longer validity); prices above are the discounted rates shown by the FindYourESIM aggregator (a site-wide promo code was applied — treat as approximate list price).
- Holafly and Maya Mobile are unlimited-only in Colombia — no capped GB tiers found. Holafly's hotspot sharing is capped at 1 GB/day even though the base plan is unshared unlimited.
- Jetpac's Colombia "Unlimited" 30-day pack ($265.50) is far more expensive than any other provider's unlimited offering — its capped per-GB packs are the more competitive option.
- GigSky: researched but no confirmed Colombia-specific pricing located as of 2026-09-17.
- No coverage caveats specific to remote Colombian regions (e.g. Amazon basin, San Andrés) were found in the sources checked.
