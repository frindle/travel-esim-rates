## Notes

- Jetpac's USA catalog has no plan smaller than 3 GB (no 1 GB tier); smallest listed row above reflects that.
- "Unlimited" plans from Airalo, Saily, Ubigi, and Jetpac are FUP-throttled (commonly 3–5 GB/day full speed, then reduced to ~1 Mbps); Holafly and Maya Mobile market flat unlimited with less explicit daily-cap disclosure.
- GigSky does not appear to sell an unlimited USA plan — fixed-GB only.
- Jetpac and GigSky Trustpilot ratings (~4.7 and ~4.3 respectively) are not yet listed in [providers.md](../providers.md) — see the Canada page for sourcing detail on those figures. Jetpac's hotspot support varies by plan per providers.md; not independently re-verified for these specific US plans, hence ⚠️ above.
