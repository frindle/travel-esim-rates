## Notes

- Airalo and Holafly's "Unlimited" plans are FUP-throttled (Airalo: 3 GB/day full speed then 1 Mbps) — not true unlimited.
- Holafly's hotspot is limited to a separate "Always On" 1 GB/day data-sharing feature, not full tethering like Saily/Ubigi.
- Movistar dominates as the resolved network across Airalo, Saily, Nomad, and Holafly — it has the strongest urban coverage (Buenos Aires, Córdoba, Rosario) per multiple reviews.
- Argentina has a history of import duties/customs friction on physical electronics and SIM cards for travelers; this does not apply to eSIMs since nothing physical is imported or declared on arrival.
- Argentina's peso has been volatile against the dollar; USD-denominated eSIM pricing (charged before arrival) avoids exposure to in-country exchange-rate swings that a local prepaid SIM purchase would carry.
- Jetpac, GigSky, and Maya Mobile figures above come from aggregator/search summaries rather than a directly fetched pricing table for the exact plan tier requested — treat as approximate and re-verify before purchase.
