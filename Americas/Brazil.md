## Carrier resolution

- **Airalo** → TIM
- **Saily** → Vivo / Claro / TIM / Oi (routes to strongest available)
- **Nomad** → TIM
- **Ubigi** → TIM + Vivo (dual, full MVNO)
- **Holafly** → Claro / TIM / Vivo
- **aloSIM** → Claro
- Jetpac, Maya Mobile, GigSky — no confirmed carrier found as of 2026-09-17.

## Notes

- Airalo's "Unlimited" plans are FUP-throttled (3 GB/day full speed, then ~1 Mbps) — not true unlimited.
- Holafly's hotspot is not full tethering: it's a separate "Always On" 1 GB/day data-sharing feature, unlike Saily/Nomad/Ubigi which support unrestricted hotspot on all plans.
- Jetpac, GigSky, and Maya Mobile figures above come from aggregator/search summaries rather than a directly fetched pricing table for the exact plan tier requested — treat as approximate and re-verify before purchase.
- Trustpilot ratings for Jetpac (~4.8) and GigSky (~4.3) are not yet in [providers.md](../providers.md) — confirmed via trustpilot.com during this research pass.
