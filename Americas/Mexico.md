## Carrier resolution

- **Nomad** → AT&T Mexico + Movistar ([Nomad Mexico](https://www.nomadesim.com/mexico-eSIM))
- **Holafly** → Movistar + Telcel ([Holafly Mexico](https://esim.holafly.com/esim-mexico/))
- **Saily** → multi-IMSI, hops between Telcel, AT&T Mexico, and Movistar depending on signal; not officially disclosed by Saily and reported to favor the weaker AT&T/Movistar networks outside major cities ([Saily Telcel eSIM guide](https://saily.com/blog/telcel-esim/))
- **Airalo** → widely reported to run on Telcel, Mexico's strongest network (per third-party comparison, not confirmed on Airalo's own site) ([Traveltomtom Mexico eSIM guide](https://www.traveltomtom.net/destinations/north-america/mexico/best-esim-cards-for-mexico))

## Notes

- Airalo's Mexico plans bundle voice minutes and SMS with data even on data-focused eSIMs (e.g. "5 GB + 50 min + 50 SMS") — factor this into cross-provider $/GB comparisons.
- "Unlimited" plans from Airalo, Saily, Ubigi, and Jetpac are FUP-throttled (commonly ~3 GB/day full speed, then ~1 Mbps); Holafly and Maya Mobile market flat unlimited with less explicit daily-cap disclosure.
- GigSky does not appear to sell an unlimited Mexico plan — fixed-GB only.
- Jetpac and GigSky Trustpilot ratings (~4.7 and ~4.3 respectively) are not yet listed in [providers.md](../providers.md) — see the Canada page for sourcing detail on those figures.
- Jetpac's hotspot support varies by plan per providers.md; not independently re-verified for these specific Mexico plans, hence ⚠️/❓ above.
