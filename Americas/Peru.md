## Notes

- Airalo's Peru catalog splits into a capped "Habla Telefon" line (1–20 GB) and a separate branded unlimited product; both are shown above.
- Holafly and Maya Mobile sell unlimited-only day-passes in Peru — no capped GB tiers found. Holafly's "unlimited" caps hotspot sharing at 1 GB/day even though the base plan itself is unshared unlimited.
- Jetpac's Peru "Unlimited" 30-day pack is priced far above other providers ($270 vs. ~$35–80 elsewhere) — its per-GB packs are the more competitive option.
- GigSky's Peru price ($7.99 for a 1 GB/7-day plan) was reported ambiguously as "$7.99/GB" in aggregator text; treated as an approximate total price pending direct confirmation on gigsky.com.
- No confirmed Machu Picchu/Cusco-specific coverage caveats were found in the sources checked; all providers above route through national Movistar/Claro networks which cover Cusco and Lima.
