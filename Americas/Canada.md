- **Holafly** → Bell + Telus ([Holafly Canada](https://esim.holafly.com/esim-canada/))
- **Nomad** → Telus Mobility + Bell ([Nomad Canada](https://www.nomadesim.com/canada-eSIM))
- **Airalo** → multi-carrier: primarily Bell LTE with 5G roaming on Bell/Telus, plus Rogers 4G/LTE ([Traveltomtom Canada eSIM guide](https://www.traveltomtom.net/destinations/north-america/canada/best-esim-cards-for-canada))
- Saily's Canada carrier resolution was not clearly disclosed in the sources checked — unconfirmed, needs follow-up.

## Notes

- Airalo, Saily, Ubigi, Nomad, aloSIM, and Jetpac all label their "Unlimited" plans as FUP-throttled (typically ~3 GB/day full speed, then ~1 Mbps) — not literally unlimited high-speed. Holafly and Maya Mobile market flat "unlimited" with less explicit daily-cap disclosure.
- Ubigi's Canada eSIMs are explicitly stated as **not usable by Canadian residents in Canada** — traveler/visitor use only.
- GigSky and Jetpac Trustpilot ratings are **not yet listed in [providers.md](../providers.md)** — found during this research: **Jetpac ~4.7** (3,500 reviews, [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/jetpacglobal.com)) and **GigSky ~4.3** (main Trustpilot listing; regional pages range 4.1–4.3, [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/gigsky.com)). Jetpac's hotspot support varies by plan (per providers.md) — not independently confirmed per-plan for Canada, hence ⚠️/❓ above.
- No confirmed pricing located for GigSky Unlimited (GigSky does not appear to sell an unlimited Canada plan) or for aloSIM's / Ubigi's exact hotspot policy on the specific plans listed above (inherited from provider-level trait, not re-verified per plan).
