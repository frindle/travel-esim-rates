## Carrier resolution

- **Airalo** → "Liberty" (Liberty Costa Rica, the network formed from Movistar Costa Rica's former assets)
- **Nomad** → dual: Kölbi (ICE, the state carrier) + Liberty
- No source above named the underlying network for Saily, Ubigi, Holafly, aloSIM, Jetpac, Maya Mobile, or GigSky in Costa Rica specifically.
- Costa Rica's three national networks are Kölbi (ICE), Movistar/Liberty, and Claro.

## Notes

- Airalo's Costa Rica catalog is **unlimited-only** in the fetched pricing (3–30 day day-passes, $19.50–$89.00) — no capped 1 GB/5 GB tier was found, the same pattern seen in Bolivia and Uruguay; a deviation from Airalo's usual per-GB model in [providers.md](../providers.md).
- Ubigi's Costa Rica pricing is unusually cheap at the top end (60 GB + unlimited-at-2 Mbps/30 d for $69, per esimdb) — the two rows above were chosen to match the 1 GB/5 GB comparison targets, not the cheapest tier.
- Nomad throttles to 512 kbps after the daily high-speed allotment is used on capped plans.
- Jetpac's per-GB Costa Rica prices reflect a September promotional discount visible on the page at fetch time (e.g. 5 GB shown as $20, "normally $23").
