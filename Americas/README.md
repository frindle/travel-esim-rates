## Notes

- No country in this region came back with zero sourced rates as of 2026-09-17.
- A few provider/country combos were deliberately omitted rather than guessed (e.g. Ubigi has no Ecuador plan page; GigSky's Panama/Guatemala/Colombia pages are JS-rendered and returned no plan data on fetch; Jetpac had no renderable Bolivia/Panama pricing). See each country page's Notes section for specifics.
- Two providers not yet in [providers.md](../providers.md)'s original table were confirmed with real Trustpilot ratings during this research pass and have since been added there: **Jetpac ~4.7** and **GigSky ~4.3**.
- Flagged anomaly: Airalo appears to sell **unlimited-only day-passes** (no capped 1GB/5GB tier) for Bolivia, Uruguay, and Costa Rica, which differs from its usual per-GB model — worth a spot-check.
