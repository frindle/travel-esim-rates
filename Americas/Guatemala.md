## Notes

- Airalo's Guatemala page only rendered "Unlimited" day-pass plans on live fetch even for fixed-GB product URLs (likely a JS-rendered tab defaulting to Unlimited); the $5.00 "from" figure comes from the page's own indexed title, and the $6.50/1GB figure comes from a MobiMatter reseller listing — both flagged ⚠️ rather than treated as confirmed line items.
- Jetpac's "Unlimited / 30 d" figure of $265.50 was the number returned on a direct fetch of Jetpac's own Guatemala product page, but it's far out of line with Jetpac's usual day-pass-style unlimited pricing seen elsewhere — treat as unverified/possibly a scrape artifact and confirm in-app before buying.
- Holafly's "unlimited" plans include a daily 1 GB hotspot-share allowance (not full unlimited hotspot) plus a bonus "Always On" 1 GB/month backup data valid across 150+ countries — confirm current FUP before buying.
- GigSky's Guatemala pricing page is JS-rendered and returned only the page title on fetch (a WebSearch summary suggested a ~$12.49–$74.99 unlimited range and per-GB bundles ~$5–$60, but none of it was independently confirmed on a fetched page), so no GigSky row was added. GigSky's Trustpilot is ~4.3 (6,645 reviews).
- Jetpac's Trustpilot is ~4.7 (3,500 reviews) — not previously listed in [providers.md](../providers.md).
- No Central America regional-bundle page specific to Guatemala was found during this pass; Saily's general "Latin America" regional plan (from $7.99) exists but a Guatemala-specific page already covers pricing, so that was used instead.
