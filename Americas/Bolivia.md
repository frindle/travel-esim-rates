## Notes

- Airalo's Bolivia catalog is **unlimited-only** in the fetched pricing (3–30 day day-passes, $19.50–$99.00) — no capped 1 GB/5 GB tier was found, which is a deviation from Airalo's usual per-GB model described in [providers.md](../providers.md); worth re-verifying if Airalo restructures.
- Jetpac has a live Bolivia product page ("Get Prepaid eSIM Data Plans for Bolivia from just $7") but the specific per-GB/duration pricing table did not render in fetched content — omitted rather than guessed. No Trustpilot rating is listed for Jetpac in providers.md (n/a, growing).
- Nomad and Ubigi both throttle rather than hard-cut after the plan allowance: Nomad drops to 512 kbps after the daily high-speed allotment, Ubigi's larger Bolivia bundles include "unlimited at 2 Mbps" after the named cap.
- Bolivia is a smaller/less-covered eSIM market overall, but all nine providers on the assigned checklist except Jetpac (pricing not extractable) yielded confirmed sourced rates.
