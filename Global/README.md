## Notes

- **Maya Mobile** and **Holafly** are the unlimited-global options; Maya includes hotspot, Holafly frequently blocks it.
- "From" prices are catalog entry points, not a specific destination — treat as a floor, verify per country.
- ❓ **"HelloRoam"** is an unconfirmed provider name (likely a mis-named/aggregate eSIMRated listing) — see [root README](../README.md#data-provenance). Its figures carry eSIMRated's cheapest-catalog numbers but the provider identity must be re-verified.
- **StaffTraveler** sells a single flat-rate product, not per-country or per-region plans: the same 5 tiers (100 MB/$0.99, 5 GB/$24, 10 GB/$38, 20 GB/$62, 50 GB/$119, all valid 365 days) were confirmed identical across its Turkey, US, China, and "Europe" landing pages and its dedicated pricing page — the per-country/per-region URLs are SEO landing pages for the same global SKU, not distinct rate cards. Hard-capped data (no throttling before the cap, top up in-app), tethering/hotspot supported on all plans, instant QR/app activation, no verified account or ID required to buy. No refund policy or Trustpilot rating was found on-site. Airline-crew-oriented branding ("For flight crew" tier). Because there is no distinct local or regional pricing, no rows were added to individual country pages or to a `_Regional.md` — everything is recorded here.
