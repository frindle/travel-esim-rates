## Notes

- Saily and Ubigi don't disclose which local carrier they resolve to on their Egypt pages ("best local network" language only) — omitted from carrier resolution rather than guessed.
- aloSIM's Egypt figures came from aloSIM's own blog post (not its live per-country pricing page, which wasn't reachable this session) — marked ❓ pending re-confirmation against the live plan page.
- Maya Mobile is unlimited-only in Egypt; no fixed-GB tiers were found, so day-pass rows are used instead of a 1 GB / 5 GB comparison.
- Jetpac's own Egypt product page doesn't name a carrier, so it's omitted from carrier resolution.
- GigSky was checked but its Egypt plan pages returned blocked/JS-rendered content that couldn't be confirmed on a fetched page — omitted rather than sourced from a search snippet alone.
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled on many destinations and hotspot is frequently blocked on its plans — confirm before buying.
