- **Nomad** → Vodacom
- **Holafly** → multi-network: Cell C, MTN, Telkom, Vodacom
- **aloSIM** → Vodacom
- **Jetpac** → reported as Vodacom by third-party aggregators; Jetpac's own product page does not name the carrier, so treat as unconfirmed

## Notes

- Saily and Ubigi don't disclose which local carrier they resolve to on their South Africa pages ("best local network" language only) — omitted from carrier resolution rather than guessed.
- Maya Mobile is unlimited-only in South Africa; no fixed-GB tiers were found, so day-pass rows are used instead of a 1 GB / 5 GB comparison.
- GigSky was checked but its South Africa plan pages returned blocked/JS-rendered content that couldn't be confirmed on a fetched page — omitted rather than sourced from a search snippet alone.
- Holafly's "unlimited" is FUP-throttled on many destinations and hotspot is frequently blocked on its plans — confirm before buying.
