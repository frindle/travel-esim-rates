## Coverage notes

- **Airalo "Africa"** — 36 countries and networks per the plan page. The page also references a
  separate **"Africa Safari"** regional option, but no plan details (countries/price) were shown on
  the fetched content — omitted rather than guessed.
- **Saily "Africa"** — 35 countries listed by name on the plan page (includes South Africa, Egypt,
  Morocco, Kenya, Nigeria, Tanzania, and others). Activates automatically on arrival in the first
  covered country, valid to purchase up to 180 days ahead.
- **Holafly "Africa"** — 28 countries, unlimited-only (no capped-GB tier), consistent with Holafly's
  unlimited-day-pass-only model documented in [providers.md](../providers.md). Includes 1 GB/month
  "backup data" per the plan page. Hotspot support is not confirmed on this specific page; marked per
  Holafly's general provider-wide hotspot-blocking trait (esp. flagged for Europe/Asia in this repo) —
  re-verify for Africa specifically before relying on it.
- **Nomad "Africa" (11 Countries Plan)** — smallest-coverage regional bundle found: Egypt, Kenya,
  Madagascar, Mauritius, Morocco, Nigeria, South Africa, Tanzania, Tunisia, Uganda, Zambia. Activates
  within 60 days of purchase.
- **Jetpac "Africa"** — the plan page did not enumerate the covered-country list inline (a "View all
  covered countries" link was referenced but not resolved); country count is therefore unconfirmed.
  The Unlimited/30-day row ($495.50) is a striking outlier vs. the capped tiers and vs. every other
  provider's Africa pricing — flagged consistent with this repo's existing caution about anomalously
  high Jetpac "Unlimited/30d" prices seen elsewhere (see [Americas/README.md](../Americas/README.md)
  notes, "$265+"). Treat as unverified/likely-miscategorized rather than a typical consumer price;
  verify directly before quoting.
- **Checked, no regional bundle found:** **aloSIM** — its Africa page confirms per-country eSIMs across
  41 countries but no single Africa-wide bundle/SKU. **Maya Mobile** — global-unlimited-only catalog
  (165+ countries); no continent-specific product exists. **Ubigi** — pricing/coverage page returned
  404 on fetch; no regional-Africa product was otherwise found. **GigSky** — not checked this pass
  (this repo's existing notes flag GigSky country pages as frequently JS-rendered/unfetchable; regional
  page likely has the same issue).
