## Notes

- Ethiopia is the thinnest market found in this Africa batch: Holafly's Ethiopia page (tried under 3 different URL patterns), Saily's, Maya Mobile's, and Ubigi's all 404'd — consistent with Ethiopia's history as a state-telecom-monopoly (Ethio Telecom) market that only opened to a second operator (Safaricom Ethiopia, launched 2022) recently. Rather than guess these providers don't support Ethiopia, they're simply omitted; a country page may exist under a URL not tried this session.
- Both providers with confirmed coverage (Airalo, Nomad) resolve to **Safaricom Ethiopia**, the newer competitive entrant — no plan found resolving to the legacy Ethio Telecom network.
- Prices per GB are noticeably higher here than in Kenya/Tanzania/Ghana/Morocco — consistent with Ethiopia's less mature international-roaming/eSIM market.
- aloSIM's page also mentioned an "Unlimited" tier with no price shown — omitted.
