- **Airalo** → China Unicom
- **Saily** → resolves across China Unicom / China Mobile / China Telecom depending on plan
- **Nomad** → China Unicom / China Telecom
- **Ubigi** → China Unicom
- **Holafly** → CMCC (China Mobile) network, with built-in VPN bundled
- **Maya Mobile** → China Unicom (4G/5G), with VPN access bundled on the China tariff
- GigSky and Jetpac's underlying mainland network was not confirmed from the pages fetched — treat as unverified until checked at purchase.

## Notes

- Mainland China is a notoriously restrictive eSIM market: the Great Firewall blocks Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, and most Western apps/VPNs by default. Some providers (Holafly, Maya Mobile) bundle a built-in VPN specifically to route around this; others (Saily) explicitly warn their own companion app may be unreachable once inside China, so set up and test the eSIM on home Wi-Fi *before* departure.
- Several providers (Airalo, Holafly) currently list mainland China as **unlimited-day-pass only** — no fixed-GB capped SKUs were found live on their sites at fetch time, unlike most other countries in this dataset.
- This page covers **mainland China only** (does not include Hong Kong, Macau, or Taiwan — see their own files in this folder). Many providers sell separate "China + HK + Macau + Taiwan" regional bundles (e.g. Jetpac's `cn-mo-hk-tw-esim`) that are out of scope here since we're pricing single-country plans.
- GigSky's own country page (gigsky.com) did not render plan pricing via fetch (likely client-side rendered); GigSky rows above are sourced from the esimdb.com aggregator instead and marked ⚠️ approx as a result.
