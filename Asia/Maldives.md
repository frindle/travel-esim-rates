## Notes

- **Maldives is by far the most expensive market in this batch** — every provider's entry-level rate is 2-4x what the same provider charges for India/Cambodia/Laos/Sri Lanka (e.g. Nomad's 1GB/7d is $17 here vs $4-6 elsewhere; Holafly's 3-day unlimited is $32.90 here vs ~$12-20 elsewhere). Reflects Dhiraagu's near-monopoly on the atolls and generally high resort-market costs.
- Airalo, Saily, and aloSIM pages each surfaced only a single plan tier on fetch (the pricing grids for other tiers appear to load dynamically/interactively and weren't captured) — reported what was confirmed rather than guessing at additional tiers.
- Maya Mobile's Maldives page returned a 404 — Maldives may not be in their covered-country list despite the "165+ countries" claim; omitted.
- Jetpac's Maldives product page did not render plan-specific pricing on fetch; omitted rather than guessing.
- Ubigi and GigSky not confirmed for Maldives; omitted rather than guessing.
- Ooredoo Maldives (the other local carrier, alongside Dhiraagu) was not found resolved to by any international eSIM provider in this research pass.
