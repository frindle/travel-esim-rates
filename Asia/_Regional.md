## Coverage notes

- **Airalo Asia (18 countries/networks)**: page lists the region as "18 Countries and Networks" but
  does not enumerate them on the fetched page — count only, not itemized. No distinct "Southeast Asia"
  Airalo product page could be located (`/southeast-asia-esim` and `/southeast-asia` both 404'd);
  Airalo may only sell the broader "Asia" bundle, not a separate SE-Asia-only one.
- **Saily "Asia" bundle (22 countries) is actually Asia *and* Oceania**: Australia, Bangladesh,
  Cambodia, China, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Japan, Kazakhstan, Laos, Macau, Malaysia, New Zealand,
  Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, Uzbekistan, Vietnam.
  Flagged here (not duplicated into Oceania/_Regional.md) because the source page brands it as its
  "Asia" plan; note the scope includes Australia/NZ.
- **Holafly Asia (16 countries)**: Cambodia, China, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Japan, Laos, Macau, Malaysia,
  Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, Vietnam. Notably excludes
  India.
- **aloSIM Asia (14 countries)**: Bangladesh, China, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Japan, Macau,
  Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, Vietnam.
- No provider's page enumerated a distinct "Southeast Asia only" (excluding China/Japan/Korea/India)
  bundle with its own pricing — all four sourced providers price a broader "Asia" region as one SKU.

## Caveats / omissions

- **aloSIM's unlimited day-pass tier is not listed above**: its Asia page's "Weekender / Extended Stay
  / Week Getaway / Traveller Plus / Great Escape / Month Abroad" unlimited tiers returned prices
  identical, dollar-for-dollar, to Airalo's Asia unlimited tiers (3d $11.50, 5d $19.00, 7d $26.00,
  10d $35.00, 15d $49.00, 30d $73.50) — consistent with aloSIM white-labeling/reselling Airalo's Asia
  eSIM for its unlimited tier (aloSIM's own capped 1–100GB plans above are distinct and kept). Omitted
  to avoid a masked duplicate row; flagged for a human to re-verify on-site rather than guessed away.
- **Maya Mobile**: no Asia-specific regional bundle found — confirmed via its coverage page that it
  sells single-country plans plus its Global unlimited tiers only (already in Global/README.md).
- **Nomad**: its regional-eSIM URL 404'd; no working Asia regional page found this pass.
- **Ubigi**: has an Asia country-selector page but it did not return plan-level pricing on fetch
  (JS-rendered) — omitted rather than guessed.
- **Jetpac**: markets "Asia from just $7" but the fetched page did not expose a plan table (JS-rendered
  or paginated) — omitted rather than guessed.
- **GigSky**: not checked this pass (known JS-rendering issue on most GigSky pages per providers.md).
- WebSearch budget was exhausted mid-research (session-wide 200-search cap already used by prior work);
  all rows above came from direct WebFetch of provider pages, not search-engine summaries.
