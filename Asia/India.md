## Notes

- **Regulatory caveat**: in Jan 2024 India's Department of Telecommunications directed Apple/Google to delist Airalo, Holafly, and other foreign eSIM apps from Indian app stores (no local NOC/licensing), and ISPs were told to block their sites for Indian-IP visitors. This affects *purchasing from within India* (app store availability, site access on an Indian connection) — it does not ban travelers from buying before departure and using the eSIM once abroad-purchased/pre-installed. Buy and install before you land. [FlyerTalk thread](https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/india/2148893-implications-airalo-holafly-ban-india.html), [esim-now explainer](https://www.esim-now.com/en/guide/airalo-alternative-india).
- Airalo's India catalog is currently **unlimited-only** (no per-GB tiers) on the VI network — confirmed live on their India page.
- Local carrier eSIMs (Airtel/Jio/Vi direct-to-consumer) require ID/address verification and 24-48h activation — not practical for short-stay tourists, so omitted here; the international travel-eSIM providers above are the tourist-friendly path.
- GigSky's India country page did not render plan pricing in the fetch (JS-loaded); omitted rather than guessing — search snippets suggested ~$2.99/GB but this was not independently confirmed on-page.
