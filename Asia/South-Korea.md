- **Nomad** → SK Telecom / KT olleh / LG U+ (multi-carrier)
- Other providers (Airalo, Saily, Holafly, aloSIM, Jetpac, Maya Mobile, GigSky) did not disclose the underlying
  Korean carrier on their plan pages fetched above — likely resolves to one of SKT/KT/LG U+ same as the others,
  but unconfirmed; re-verify before relying on it.
