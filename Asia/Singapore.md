## Notes

- Local telcos (Singtel, StarHub, M1) sell physical tourist prepaid SIMs at Changi Airport, but as of this research **Singtel's own eSIM tourist product ("hi!") is online-order only** — airport counters are physical-SIM only. Priced in SGD; converted to USD at ~0.786 (2026-09-15 spot).
- Jetpac has **no single-country Singapore plan** — only Southeast Asia regional bundles (covering Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia). Row above is the regional plan, not Singapore-exclusive.
- GigSky's Singapore per-GB pricing is notably higher than the other providers ($9–10/GB) — included for completeness, not recommended on price.
- Ubigi's Smartstart plans don't activate until arrival, so validity windows shown start on landing, not purchase.
