## Notes

- Japan is the best-covered destination in the seed data; Nomad is called out as best value/GB in APAC.

- Nomad's Japan-specific 5 GB/30-day price is confirmed direct from nomadesim.com at **$10.00 ($2.00/GB)** —
  the earlier "~$1.02 (APAC regional)" row is a different (regional-bucket) plan and is kept above for reference,
  but the direct Japan plan above is the more reliable figure.
- Maya Mobile and Jetpac only offer GB-capped/unlimited bundles starting at 5 GB for Japan (no confirmed 1 GB tier found for either).
